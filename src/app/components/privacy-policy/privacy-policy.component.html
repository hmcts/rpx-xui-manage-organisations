{{ 'Applying for divorce' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'When you apply for divorce on behalf of a client we ask for your:' | rpxTranslate }}

  • {{ 'name' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'email address' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'phone number' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'role or position' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ "firm's address" | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'We also ask for your client’s personal data, which includes:' | rpxTranslate }}

  • {{ 'the applicant\'s current name' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the respondent\'s current name' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'proof of the applicant name change if they\'ve changed it since they got married' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'a copy of the marriage certificate and the details from it' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the country in which the applicant and respondent are habitually resident in England or Wales' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'whether the applicant and respondent are habitually resident in England or Wales' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'whether the applicant\'s and respondent\'s domicile is in England or Wales' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the applicant\'s email address' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the applicant\'s phone number' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the applicant\'s address' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the respondent\'s address' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the address of the respondent\'s solicitor (if they have one)' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'details around the reason for the divorce' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'details of other court cases the applicant and respondent have been involved in' | rpxTranslate }}
  • {{ 'the name and address of the person who allegedly committed adultery with the respondent (this is optional and only asked if adultery is the reason for divorce)' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'Receiving notifications' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'You need to sign up to receive notifications. This is a legal requirement so that the divorce application can proceed.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'Storing your data' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'While you are filling out or responding to a divorce application we will hold your data for up to 6 months. If you don’t complete the application during this time you’ll have to start again.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'When a divorce is finalised the case is stored for 18 years. After this time, some data (from the decree nisi and the decree absolute) is deleted.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'The remainder of the case information is stored for an additional 82 years. After a total of 100 years this data will be deleted.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'When you use this service you’ll be asked to use your email address to set up an account. You can use this email and password to sign into other HMCTS services.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'Before you submit your information' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'The information you enter in the money claims service is saved until you decide to submit it. This allows you to save what you are doing and continue later. Saved information that you do not submit will be deleted after 90 days.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'After you submit your information' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'The information you submit for a money claim will be deleted 2 years after the court makes a decision on the outcome of your claim (this is called a judgment).' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'The information you submit will be shared with everyone who is named on the claim. This excludes any payment details that you use to pay court fees.' | rpxTranslate }}

{{ 'If a judgment is made on a case some information is shared with Registry Trust Limited who provide financial information about court judgments to banks and credit agencies.' | rpxTranslate }}
